Clip Uploader

Clip Uploader is a personal tool used by its owner to publish short vertical videos (clips) that they created to their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts at scheduled times.

The tool runs only on the owner's computer. It is not offered to the public and it does not manage other people's accounts.

What it does

  • Uploads finished clips with title, description and hashtags written by the owner.
  • Publishes them at the time chosen by the owner.
  • Uses only the official APIs of YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, with the account owner's permission (OAuth).
