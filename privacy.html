Privacy Policy – Clip Uploader

Last updated: 24 November 2026

1. Who we are

Clip Uploader is a personal tool, run by a single individual on their own computer, to publish their own videos to their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

2. Data we access

With the account owner's explicit permission (OAuth), the tool accesses only what is needed to publish videos:

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos (scope youtube.upload).
  • Instagram: basic profile information and permission to publish content (instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish).
  • TikTok: basic profile information and permission to upload and publish videos (user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).

3. How we use the data

The data is used only to upload and publish the owner's videos to the owner's own accounts. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

4. Where the data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's local computer. The tool has no server and does not collect data from other users.

5. Google API Services

Clip Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time:

  • Google/YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services

When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the local computer.
